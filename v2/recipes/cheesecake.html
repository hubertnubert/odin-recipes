The World's Easiest Cheesecake.
This cheesecake only requires a few ingredients and minimal effort.
It's perfect for those who want a delicious dessert without the hassle of baking.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg low-fat curd cheese
  • 125 g of butter
  • 250 g of sugar
  • 3-4 eggs
  • 2 tablespoons of semolina
  • 8 g of baking powder
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 1 teaspoon of vanilla sugar

Steps

  1. Mix everything together and bake for about 70 minutes at 160°C (fan oven).
  2. Done.
