Quick Warm Vanilla Cream.
I love pudding for dessert! Homemade always tastes best—especially when served slightly warm..
If you want to take this creamy classic to the next level, try adding thickened cherries, cookie crumbles, whipped cream, or any of your other favorite toppings.
Simply heavenly and so quick to make!

Ingredients

  • 1 vanilla bean
  • 500 ml of milk
  • 25 g of cornstarch
  • 1 egg
  • 75 g of sugar

Steps

  1. Slice the vanilla bean lengthwise and scrape out the seeds with the back of a knife.
  2. Bring 400 ml of milk, the vanilla seeds, and the bean to a boil.
  3. In a separate bowl, whisk together the cornstarch, egg, sugar, and the remaining 100 ml of milk until smooth.
  4. Whisk the starch mixture into the boiling milk and let it simmer for about 1 minute while stirring constantly.
  5. Remove the vanilla bean and serve the cream warm.
